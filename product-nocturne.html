Movement and finishing

A self-winding movement with small seconds at six, regulated by the house in five positions before it is cased. The bridges carry Geneva stripes, and the rotor turns behind the sapphire caseback.

Delivery and returns

Insured hand delivery worldwide within five working days, in a signed box. If it is not right, return it unworn within 30 days for a full refund.

Warranty and service

Five years on the movement. The first service is carried out by the house, and every watch can be brought to the Mayfair salon for servicing.

Engraving and straps

Engraving is cut by hand on the caseback and adds two working days. Every strap has quick-release bars, so you can change it in seconds without a tool.
